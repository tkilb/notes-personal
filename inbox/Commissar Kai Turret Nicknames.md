# Commissar Kai's Sentry / Turret Nicknames

Nicknames popularized by *Helldivers 2* content creator **Commissar Kai** (from his guides including *"Use Sentries Like an Expert"*):

## Core Direct-Fire Sentries

- **Mathilda** (or **Matilda**) – `A/MG-43 Machine Gun Sentry`
  - The dependable, low-cooldown workhorse sentry.
- **Gerry** (or **Gary**) – `A/G-16 Gatling Sentry`
  - High fire rate chaff shredder; affectionately notorious for mowing down teammates in sweep arcs.
- **Andy** (or **Amanda**) – `A/AC-8 Autocannon Sentry`
  - Heavy anti-armor specialist for priority medium-to-heavy targets.
- **Randy** – `A/MLS-4X Rocket Sentry`
  - Long-range anti-tank and aerial target suppression.

## Artillery & Support Emplacements

- **Morty** – `A/M-12 Mortar Sentry`
  - Indirect high-explosive saturation bombardment.
- **Rick** – `A/M-23 EMS Mortar Sentry`
  - Area-of-effect static shock crowd control field (often paired with Morty as the "Rick and Morty" combo).
- **Burnice** – `A/FLAM-40 Flame Sentry`
  - Area denial flame turret for bug breaches and choke points.
- **Terry** – `FX-12 Tesla Tower`
  - Static arc discharge tower.
- **Larry** – Laser Sentry / Emplacement
  - Continuous beam directed energy turret.
